02 — The pour

Three states of one cup.

Scroll to move through the brew. The stage is scrubbed frame by frame, never played — your thumb sets the clock.

01 — Raw ingredients

Sourced with intention.

Direct relationships, harvested at peak ripeness, rested after travel before they ever meet the drum.

02 — Mid-brew

Roasted to order.

Twelve-kilo batches, profiled by ear and eye. Nothing ships that was roasted more than four days ago.

03 — Finished pour

Brewed with care.

Ninety-four degrees, four pours, three minutes. Ceramics warmed first, because the cup remembers cold.
